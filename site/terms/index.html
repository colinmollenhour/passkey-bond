The software

Passkey.bond is open-source software released under the MIT licence. The licence governs your use of the code. Nothing on this page limits the rights it gives you.

Your vault is your responsibility

Your passkeys and codes are stored only on your devices. We can't see them, recover them or reset access to them. Keep your sync phrase or an encrypted backup somewhere safe. If you lose every device and have neither, your vault cannot be recovered by anyone, including us.

Phone app purchases

The browser extension is free. The phone apps are a one-time purchase through Google Play or the App Store, US$2.95 in the United States (each store sets the price in other countries), and your purchase includes future updates to the app. Refunds follow the store's own refund policy.

Sync relays

Sync uses public relays run by third parties. We don't operate them and can't guarantee they stay online. Your devices keep working without them; they just can't sync until a relay is available.
