Chrome

Free

Also Edge, Brave, Opera and other Chromium browsers.

Chrome Web Store: coming soon

Until the store listing is live, install it from GitHub:

  1. Download passkey-bond-chrome.zip and unzip it.
  2. Open chrome://extensions and turn on Developer mode.
  3. Click Load unpacked and choose the unzipped folder.
Download for Chrome

Firefox

Free

Firefox 109 and newer, on your computer.

Firefox Add-ons: coming soon

Until it's listed on Firefox Add-ons, you can try it from GitHub as a temporary add-on:

  1. Download passkey-bond-firefox.zip and unzip it.
  2. Open about:debugging#/runtime/this-firefox.
  3. Click Load Temporary Add-on and choose manifest.json. Firefox removes it when it restarts.
Download for Firefox
